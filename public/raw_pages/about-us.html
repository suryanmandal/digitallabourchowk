About Us Digital Labour Chowk

Welcome to Digital Labour Chowk, India’s 1st job platform for Blue-Collar Worker in the Construction industry. We revolutionize the hiring process by connecting businesses with skilled blue-collar worker and professionals through our innovative platform.

India’s Blue-Collar Hiring Platform

Digital Labour Chowk

Digital Labour Chowk is a workforce platform that helps blue-collar workers find jobs and connects them with trusted contractors and employers across India. The platform makes hiring simple and removes the need for middlemen, so workers get fair and reliable job opportunities in the informal and unorganised sectors.

Using digital tools and a people-first approach, Digital Labour Chowk supports workers in finding stable work and helps contractors hire the right people quickly. The platform works across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 cities, creating better access to jobs and improving income security for workers.

The journey of building Digital Labour Chowk and its positive impact on job creation and worker empowerment have been featured on Bharat Ke Super Founders. This recognition highlights the platform’s role in improving blue-collar employment and creating a more fair and transparent labour system in India.

A day without work means a day without food—this struggle is real for millions.

About Digital Labour Chowk

Founded in 2020 by Chandrashekhar Mandal, Digital Labour Chowk was born out of the urgent need to bring structure, fairness, and opportunity to the lives of blue collar workers across India. The platform was designed to solve the hiring challenges in the construction sector by directly linking skilled labour with companies in need of reliable manpower. By removing middlemen and bringing technology to the forefront, we ensure that blue collar workers get timely job access, transparent wages, and dignity in work.

Our mission is to empower India’s unorganised workforce by making job opportunities accessible, streamlined, and trustworthy. With every worker onboarded, we move closer to our goal of building a future where blue collar workers are valued, visible, and vital contributors to India’s growth story.

Our Mission

To empower blue collar workers by creating a trusted, tech-driven job platform that ensures easy, fair, and direct access to employment opportunities. We aim to solve the hiring challenges in the construction and informal labour sectors by connecting skilled workers with businesses that value reliability, transparency, and human dignity.

Our Vision

To become India’s most impactful employment platform for blue collar workers, where every skilled labourer—regardless of background or location—can access dignified, stable work. We envision a future where no daily wage earner is left behind, and every company has access to a verified, dependable workforce that fuels real growth and inclusion.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Digital Labour Chowk?

Digital Labour Chowk is an online platform that connects contractors, construction companies, and other employers with verified blue-collar worker across India. It simplifies the process of hiring skilled, semi-skilled, and unskilled labour for short-term and long-term projects.

 
How can contractors or companies hire workers through the platform?

Contractors and companies can register on the platform, post their job requirements, and instantly get access to a pool of verified worker based on location, skill, and availability. The hiring process is fast, simple, and fully digital.

 
What kind of workers are available on Digital Labour Chowk?

The platform offers a wide range of blue-collar worker, including masons, carpenters, electricians, plumbers, painters, welders, helpers, drivers, and ITI-certified technicians, among others.

Is there a fee for workers or companies to use the platform?

Registration for worker is completely free. Employers may have to pay a minimal service fee depending on the scale and nature of their hiring needs. For bulk hiring or long-term contracts, customized plans are available.
