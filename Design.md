# Design Guidelines & System

## 1. Colour and Theme

The visual design system of **Digital Labour Chowk** reflects trust, professionalism, and industry authority in civil construction and workforce management.

### Primary Palette
- **DLC Royal Blue (`#0C59D4`)**: Primary brand color. Used for active navigation pills, primary CTA buttons, badges, key headlines, and brand accents.
- **DLC Navy Dark (`#083B8F` / `#0A1E3F`)**: Deep navy for header elements, hover states, and enterprise cards.
- **DLC Light Blue (`#EEF4FF` / `#F0F6FF`)**: Subtle background tint for pill badges, category cards, and subtle highlights.

### Secondary & Semantic Accents
- **Amber / Safety Orange (`#FF7A00` / `#F59E0B`)**: Construction identity accent for safety vests, warning highlights, and urgent CTAs.
- **Success Emerald (`#10B981` / `#059669`)**: Used for verified badges, phone numbers, Aadhaar verification checkmarks, and active status indicators.
- **Dark Neutral (`#090D16` / `#0F172A`)**: Primary text color for high-contrast readability.
- **Light Neutral (`#F8FAFC` / `#FFFFFF`)**: Base page backgrounds and clean card containers.
- **Border Slate (`#E2E8F0` / `#CBD5E1`)**: Crisp borders for clean section division.

---

## 2. Fonts

- **Primary Typeface**: `Inter`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`.
- **Characteristics**: Crisp geometric neo-grotesque sans-serif with high legibility on mobile screens and outdoor high-glare environments.
- **Devanagari Font Support**: Fallback to system Hindi fonts (`Noto Sans Devanagari`, `Kohinoor Devanagari`, `Mangal`) for bilingual trade nomenclature (*राजमिस्त्री, इलेक्ट्रीशियन, बढ़ई*).

---

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

### Headings
- **H1 (Hero Display)**: `text-4xl sm:text-5xl lg:text-6xl`, `font-black`, `tracking-tight`, `leading-[1.12]` — Bold, authoritative presentation for hero value statements.
- **H2 (Section Titles)**: `text-3xl sm:text-4xl`, `font-extrabold`, `text-slate-900`, `tracking-tight` — Clear thematic titles.
- **H3 (Card Headings)**: `text-xl sm:text-2xl`, `font-bold`, `text-slate-900` — Feature and category titles.
- **H4 (Subheadings & Labels)**: `text-base sm:text-lg`, `font-semibold`, `text-slate-800`.

### Body Text
- **Lead Paragraphs**: `text-base sm:text-lg`, `text-slate-600`, `leading-relaxed`, `max-w-xl`.
- **Standard Body**: `text-sm sm:text-base`, `text-slate-600`, `leading-relaxed`.
- **Small / Metadata**: `text-xs`, `text-slate-500`, `font-medium`.

### Badges & Micro-Copy
- **Pill Badges**: `text-xs`, `font-bold`, `uppercase`, `tracking-wider`, `px-3.5 py-1.5`, `rounded-full`.
- **Button Labels**: `text-sm`, `font-bold` or `font-black`, `tracking-wide`.
