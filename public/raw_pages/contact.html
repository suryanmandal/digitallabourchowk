FAQ's

What is Digital Labour Chowk?

Digital Labour Chowk is an online platform that Connects Contractors / Companies with daily wage workers regarding their demands and needs Digital Labour Chowk facilitate the process of hiring and finding work opportunities.

Why to choose Digital Labour Chowk?

Digital Labour Chowk allows you to find a job as per your skills from the comfort of your home. No need to sit on Labour Chowk to find a Job

How to find a job in Digital Labour Chowk?

Getting a job on Digital Labour Chowk is very simple just Download our Digital Labour Rojgar App and register yourself by filling appropriate details ( NAME, Mobile No. , Gender, Skills, Daily Wage ) Digital Labour Chowk provides a user-friendly interface where you can browse and search for job listings posted by contractors explore available jobs, and apply to the ones that match your skills.

How can workers get paid for their services?

The payment process is handled directly between the contractor and the worker. Digital Labour Chowk facilitates the connection, but the payment terms and method are agreed upon by both parties. Payment Methods can be cash or Digital both.

What steps are taken to ensure the safety of workers on platform?

Digital Labour Chowk prioritizes the safety of its users. The platform verifies contractor profiles and encourages workers to share feedback about their experiences, creating a secure environment for everyone involved.

Is their any cost associated with using Digital Labour Chowk as a worker?

This platform is absolutely free for daily wage workers there is no cost for workers to use the platform. Registration and job application are free of charge.
